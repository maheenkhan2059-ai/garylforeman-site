About Gary

He sees everything on a larger scale — and built a career on it.

An award-winning producer, director, author, and photographer, Gary has shaped documentary television and Emmy-nominated storytelling. His monumental EPICArt® murals have transformed public and corporate spaces, setting a new standard for Sacred Ground.

Raised in the Midwest, Gary started in media at an ABC affiliate while still in college. In Texas, he found the stories and landscapes worth a lifetime. His vision for Alamo Plaza arrived decades ahead of its redevelopment, and his murals now greet travelers on the highways to San Jacinto Battlefield.

His new book, Finding David Crockett: The Search for the Lion of the West, brings it all together.

Gary and his wife, Laurie, a Crockett descendant, live in Granbury, Texas.

The New Book

Finding David Crockett: The Search for the Lion of the West

Everyone knows the legend. Almost no one knows the man.

Behind the coonskin cap, the stage plays, and the Hollywood myth was a real person: a farmer, soldier, politician, husband, and father who lived by his wits and died with courage. Gary L. Foreman set out to find him.

Part history and part journey, this illustrated book combines Gary’s writing with his photography of the places where Crockett lived, fought, and fell. QR codes on the pages take you to videos Gary shot on location, where he expands the story while you’re still reading it. You don’t just read about Crockett’s world. You walk through it with the author.

One Man’s Obsession with the Real David Crockett

Most authors study history from a desk. Gary Foreman went looking for it.

Nearly five decades of reenacting. More than 25,000 miles on Crockett’s trails. Massive murals across Texas, including Washington-on-the-Brazos and beside the Alamo. His work has taken him to the Biography Channel and History as a Crockett expert. Finding David Crockett is where all those miles finally come together.

The Lion of the West is waiting. Come find him.

Speaking Engagements

Making the past feel immediate

As an Emmy-nominated filmmaker, photographer, and author, Gary brings audiences face to face with the people and places that shaped America — and shows why they still matter.

01

Quantum Theory and History, Joined Together

What if time and space aren’t what they seem? Gary makes the case that human energy reveals them as an illusion, and that history is the evidence. Once you learn to see it, you will never look at yesterday, or today, the same way.

02

Connecting with the Original American Mind

The Founders created a 5,000-year leap into something the world had long sought and never achieved — meant to be shared widely for “those millions yet to come.” Gary shows how to reconnect with the thinking behind that vision.

03

Connecting the Past to the Present

History isn’t behind us. It’s the ground we stand on. Gary shows how the stories of the past explain who we are now and where we are headed.
